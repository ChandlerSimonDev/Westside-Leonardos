For My Family

Every burger that goes out that window, every late night and every early morning, all of it is for you.

People see a takeout. I see the place that kept a roof over our heads and let me watch you all grow up.

Thank you for your patience on the nights I wasn't home, for pitching in when it got busy, and for being the reason I keep going. Whatever you choose to do in life, I'm proud of every one of you.

This place has our name on it because it belongs to all of us.

With love, Dad
